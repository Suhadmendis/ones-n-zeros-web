Custom Enterprise Software

Build systems tailored to your organization's unique processes and operational needs.

Rather than forcing your business into a generic platform, we build systems that match the way your organization actually works — scalable as you grow and easy for your team to adopt.

This is our core service: a system built specifically around how your teams actually operate, not a generic template retrofitted to fit. A typical engagement covers requirements discovery, system architecture and design, development, integration with the tools you already rely on, deployment, and support once the system is live. It's the right fit for organizations that have outgrown spreadsheets and email chains, or whose off-the-shelf software no longer keeps pace with how the business has grown.

As a software development company based in Sri Lanka, we work with organizations across manufacturing, education, and logistics — sectors where a badly fitted system doesn't just slow people down, it costs real money. Whether you're a growing company near Colombo or operating across multiple sites island-wide, the goal is the same: software that fits your business, not the other way around.

Business Automation

Automate repetitive processes and increase operational efficiency.

We identify the manual, time-consuming tasks within your operations and replace them with reliable automated workflows, freeing your team to focus on higher-value work.

Automation engagements usually start with a process audit — mapping where staff time is being lost to manual data entry, repetitive approvals, duplicate reporting, or systems that don't talk to each other. From there we design and build automated workflows that handle the repetitive part reliably, with proper logging and oversight, so nothing falls through the cracks the way it can with a process managed across spreadsheets and email.

Business automation is often the fastest way for a Sri Lankan company to see measurable operational impact, particularly for organizations running finance, procurement, HR, or logistics processes that haven't kept pace with the size the business has grown into. It's also a natural complement to custom enterprise software: many of our automation projects start as a standalone fix and grow into a broader system as the value becomes clear.

Corporate Website Development

Professional websites designed for credibility, visibility, and growth.

We design and build fast, credible websites that represent your business professionally and support your visibility and growth online.

A corporate website is often the first real impression a prospective client, partner, or investor has of your business, and for many Sri Lankan companies it's still treated as an afterthought. We approach it as engineering, not just design: fast-loading, mobile-first, built on clean code rather than bloated page builders, and structured from the outset for search visibility — the technical foundation that determines whether prospective customers can actually find you online.

Every website we build includes a content structure that reflects how your business genuinely communicates value and is easy for your own team to update after launch. For businesses based in Colombo and across Sri Lanka competing for attention online, the difference between a generic template and a properly engineered site is often what separates credibility from doubt in a prospective client's mind.

AI-Powered Solutions

Leverage intelligent technologies to improve business operations and decision-making.

We help organizations adopt AI-driven automation and insights in a practical, business-first way — improving decision-making without unnecessary complexity.

Rather than chasing AI as a trend, we focus on where it creates measurable value inside a specific business process: intelligent automation that reduces manual review and data entry, reporting tools that surface patterns your team would otherwise miss, and interfaces that make complex internal systems easier for non-technical staff to use day to day.

Every system we design is built AI-ready from the architecture up, so intelligent features can be introduced incrementally as the business need becomes clear, rather than requiring a rebuild later. For Sri Lankan businesses evaluating AI adoption, that practical, business-first approach tends to matter more than the technology itself — the goal is better decisions and less manual work, not AI for its own sake.

Logistics & Fleet Management Solutions

Industry-specific solutions for transport and logistics organizations.

Our flagship Transport Management System gives transport and fleet operators centralized visibility across vehicles, drivers, trips, and operations.

This is where our engineering background runs deepest. Our flagship product grew out of hands-on experience building GPS tracking and fleet management systems for large-scale transport operations — including a deployment for a taxi operator running a fleet of several hundred vehicles — and that operational knowledge shaped how the Transport Management System is designed today.

For transport and logistics operators in Sri Lanka, the platform brings trip management, driver oversight, vehicle tracking, and reporting into a single system, replacing the fragmented mix of paper logs, spreadsheets, and disconnected tools many operators still rely on. It's built for organizations that need real-time visibility across a fleet, not just historical reports after the fact.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the custom software development process look like?

We start by understanding your organization's actual processes and operational needs, then design and build a system around how your business really works — rather than forcing you into a generic, one-size-fits-all platform. The specific steps, milestones, and timeline for your project are scoped together during your consultation, based on the size and complexity of what you need.

Do you work with existing systems, or only build from scratch?

Every engagement is scoped around your specific situation — building a new system where that makes sense, or working within what you already have where it doesn't. What's right for your organization is something we work out together during your consultation.

What industries do you build for?

We work across industries. Our core services — custom enterprise software, business automation, corporate websites, and AI-powered solutions — are built to fit whatever your organization needs, and we also have particular depth in transport and logistics through our flagship Transport Management System.

How is enterprise software different from off-the-shelf tools like generic SaaS?

Off-the-shelf and generic SaaS tools ask your business to adapt to their structure. We do the opposite: we build systems that match the way your organization actually works, so they scale as you grow and are easy for your team to adopt, instead of forcing a compromise.

What happens after launch — is there ongoing support?

Yes. Our support team remains available after your system goes live at support@onesnzeros.net. The specific scope of ongoing support and maintenance for your project is agreed as part of your engagement.
